## Numerical equivalence

`experiments/numerics.py` compares original and conjugated linear projections using deterministic 32→64 matrices. FP32 maximum error is about 9.54e-6. BF16 maximum deviation from original FP32 is about 3.97e-2, FP16 about 5.00e-3, and FP8 E4M3 about 5.80e-1. These are single-layer synthetic measurements, not model-wide quality bounds.

An early experiment multiplied by the output basis twice and produced implausible errors. A regression test now verifies the experiment itself, including separate reduced-precision error against original FP32.

## Fortress F0 and M0

Four independently spawned local workers execute real Rust QUIC role calls. F0 completes dense prefill, cached decode, and sampling with zero error against the fixed-point reference across 168 inference role calls. M0 completes two synthetic DiT steps with zero fixed-point error across 328 inference role calls. Their reports also include original-model deltas, frame bytes, mixed decoys, canary revocation, collusion measurements, zero local cost, and zero worker exit codes. These are local CPU results, not physical multi-host or GPU validation. See `docs/evidence/fortress/f0.json` and `m0.json` for exact measurements.

The mounted laboratory uses exact BigInt role products and prime-field verification. It caught and corrected a lossy JavaScript `number` conversion above 2^53, illustrating why simulations must be tested against the same arithmetic contract as production.
