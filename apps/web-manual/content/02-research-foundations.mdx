# 02. Research Foundations

<Evidence type="published">Primary research and standards</Evidence>

Xurcroh combines established engineering mechanisms rather than introducing a new cryptographic hardness assumption. ConjFormer studies orthogonally equivariant transformer inference. CryptoMoE motivates balanced expert dispatch. QUIC supplies independent streams with TLS 1.3, while RFC 8439 specifies ChaCha20-Poly1305 authenticated encryption.

## Important distinctions

Fully homomorphic encryption and secure multiparty computation offer different confidentiality guarantees from basis obfuscation. They do not justify describing a rotation as non-reversible. Hardware trusted execution environments offer another boundary, but physical memory-bus attacks and attestation-forgery risks mean that hardware quotes are evidence, not absolute truth.
