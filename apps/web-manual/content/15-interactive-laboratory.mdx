# 15. Interactive Laboratory

<Evidence type="local">Deterministic simulations validated by automated tests</Evidence>

Every lab computes its displayed state from explicit inputs. Playback changes the current state, not a decorative timer. Step forward/backward, reset, parameters, and speed are functional controls. Reduced-motion preferences disable autoplay, and expensive FWHT work can move to a Web Worker.

## Laboratories

- [Conjugation](#conjugation): original/transformed matrices, recovered output, equivalence error, and norm leakage.
- [FWHT](#fwht): every butterfly stage and direct-matrix comparison.
- [Pipeline](#pipeline): discrete events for encrypted messages, failure, retry, and cleanup.
- [Onion](#onion): actual ECDH/AES-GCM layers, per-hop visibility, tamper, and replay.
- [MoE](#moe): router logits, expert selection, dummy padding, and output combination.
- [Diffusion/video](#diffusion): latent rotation, Euler steps, permutation, and recurrent state.
- [Memory](#memory): allocation, release, synchronization, zeroization, and reuse.

These are teaching models, not production inference substitutes. No chart reports a hardware benchmark unless explicitly labeled as measured on physical equipment.
